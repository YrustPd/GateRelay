# Code of Conduct

GateRelay aims to be a focused and respectful open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be professional and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but keep technical claims concrete.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private keys, tokens, or sensitive logs.
- Keep discussions relevant to GateRelay and its documented security model.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials, private keys, full tokens, or exploit details.
- Repeatedly pushing changes that would make GateRelay an open proxy after maintainers have rejected that direction.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or delete content that violates this code of conduct, and may restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its users.
